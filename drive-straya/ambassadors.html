Kylie Rixon

Owner operator · Sunset Tourist Park

Mount Isa · Outback Queensland

I have red dirt running through my veins, that’s how passionate I am about Outback Queensland. I am based in Mount Isa, where you can go from sand dunes in the south, to the gulf country in the north, world class attractions in the east and knocking on the door of the NT just 200 km to the west. If you are looking for adventures that will stay with you for a lifetime, come and see the other side of Queensland.

Richard Kinnon

Founder · Outback Pioneers

Longreach · Outback Queensland

My passion for all things outback and Australian drives everything I do, especially through our award-winning Outback Pioneers tourism business in Longreach. For me, the greatest reward is seeing our city cousins come out here to reconnect with their heritage and experience the outback spirit. I’m always up for a yarn, and I’m deeply committed to putting Outback Queensland on the map.

Nadine Lorenz

Manager, visitor services and membership · Mackay Isaac Tourism

Mackay · Mackay Isaac

I am a proud Mackay local. I spent eight years with Diamantina Shire Council in Birdsville, and I’m now back in Mackay with Mackay Isaac Tourism. The region runs from pristine beaches and Eungella rainforest to the Isaac mining country. As an ambassador I point travellers to drive itineraries that show the best of it.

Brooke Turner

Manager · Broadwater Tourist Park

Gold Coast · Gold Coast

When I was six, my parents took our family of six into caravan park management on the Gold Coast. I grew up in the caravanning industry, graduated with a double degree in business and international tourism and hotel management in 2022, and in 2023 took a business development role with the Caravan Park Association of Queensland. I now manage Broadwater Tourist Park with my partner.

Patrice Brandenburg

Director, communities · Diamantina Shire Council

Birdsville · Outback Queensland

I’m proud to represent the Diamantina Shire in far western Queensland. I arrived in Birdsville in 2023 after more than 20 years in major events, and fell for the town and the 94,000 square kilometres around it — the gateway to Munga-Thirri–Simpson Desert National Park. I help travellers plan drives through Birdsville, Bedourie and Betoota.

Glenda Daly

Manager, business development and strategy · Cobbold Gorge

Forsayth · Far North Queensland

I grew up on a wheat and sheep farm in central-west New South Wales. Six years as an overland driver and tour leader in Africa set the course, then resort management, six years in Longreach, and from 2014 Cobbold Gorge — first as marketing manager, then general manager. I’ve worked in the industry for 30 years and I’m glad to be an ambassador for the state, the regions and the destinations.
