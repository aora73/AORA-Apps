Saturday tides – 2027
Every Saturday in 2027. The low tide, and the high tide that comes after it.

Daylight low: Every low ≤ 0.50 m during daylight (≈06:00–17:30 AEST).
Location: K’gari · 75 Mile Beach (open coast on the east side of K’gari). Use this for 75 Mile Beach and the rocky headlands.
Note: Rainbow Beach has no Bureau gauge — use this open-coast data, not Urangan, Kingfisher Bay or Tin Can Bay.
