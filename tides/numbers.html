How these figures were made

Heights are above Lowest Astronomical Tide. Predictions are © Bureau of Meteorology via Maritime Safety Queensland.

The Saturday listings were compiled for K’gari’s open coast, 75 Mile Beach. They are not Urangan, Kingfisher Bay or Tin Can Bay predictions. Times, heights, rise durations, daylight-low flags, public holiday notes and adjacent tides come from that station’s published predictions.

Best daylight lows called out on the sheet, including 23 Jan (0.40 m), 20 Feb (0.43 m) and 5 Jun (0.42 m), are the lowest daylight lows in that compiled year. Always verify beach conditions on the day. A prediction is not a permit to drive.
